# Why ads.txt is unchanged

`public/ads.txt` is left as a comment-only file:

> ads.txt - no authorised Google sellers. Adsterra does not require ads.txt entries.

Weekly Meal Planner serves display ads through Adsterra, not Google AdSense or other
IAB sellers that require a publisher `ads.txt` line. Adsterra does not require
seller entries in ads.txt. Adding invented Google seller rows would be inaccurate.
The file therefore stays a short notice and is not expanded in this change.
